Egocentric soccer players: watch through their eyes

Each red player is controlled by a policy that sees only its own camera: low-resolution pixels from its eyes, plus its own body sense, the match clock and score, and what teammates say or gesture. No positions, no map. The blue team is GRFBot, a scripted opponent with full knowledge of the game state.

Pick a game. Auto follows the red player closest to the ball. Click any player below to watch the game from their eyes instead. The big view is the player's widest eye camera re-rendered at high resolution with a slightly smoothed gaze (display only). The small pixelated image is exactly what the policy receives: 96 x 96 pixels, 10 times a second. Each clip is one illustrative game, not an evaluation.

What you are seeing

  • 11v11 on a full pitch, simulated in MuJoCo-Warp. Each clip is the first 60 seconds of a game.
  • Red: a pixel-only policy trained by imitation of a full-state teacher, then reinforcement learning with self-play, past versions and GRFBot as opponents. Each player runs the same network on its own camera stream.
  • The policy's eye has four nested views (wide 120 degrees down to a 4 degree fovea); only the widest is shown. Black corners are outside the field of view, which narrows at sprint speed.
  • Games 1-3: the current best balanced checkpoint (in a 32-game evaluation against GRFBot: 11 wins, 20 draws, 1 loss). Game 4: a variant trained without the imitation term, which scores more but passes less.
  • Results are against GRFBot only, and each clip is a single sampled game.
