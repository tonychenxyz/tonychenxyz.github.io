How to read this

JEV edges are pairs JEV was actually asked about; the number is its probability of “yes”. Pairs were picked at random, so a missing edge means “not asked”, not “unrelated”.

Inferred edges link paragraphs through a chain of JEV edges. A chain's strength is its edges multiplied together, times 0.7 for each extra step, up to 4 steps.

Embedding lists the nearest paragraphs by embedding similarity (Qwen3-Embedding-4B), the usual kind of search, shown for comparison.
