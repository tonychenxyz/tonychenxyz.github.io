About this evaluation

Luna xhigh wrote the rubrics. Luna high judged all six models. Astra, Sol, Opus and Grok answered at xhigh effort; Gemini 3.8 Flash at high and Kimi K3 at max. No model browsed or used tools.

Each rubric has one primary cognitive-pattern category, assigned editorially from what it actually tests. Factual recall is kept separate. These describe the contribution being tested, not a model's hidden reasoning.

Categories do not change judgments. Scores measure coverage of the selected reference ideas, not overall intelligence.

ASR and speaker labels may contain errors. Provider instructions differ between CLI and API runs. Turns without rubrics do not enter pass rates. Gemini and Kimi predictions were skipped on these turns.
